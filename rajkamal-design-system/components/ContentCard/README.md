# ContentCard

A bordered panel for content pages: office addresses, FAQ groups, policy blocks and form side panels.

**The consumer provides** a heading (`card-heading`) and body text or links.

**Rules**
- `surface-content` fill, 1px `border-content`, `radius-panel` 18px, `space-24` padding (`space-18` for form side cards).
- Hover (only when the whole card is a link): the border turns `accent` and `shadow-card-hover` appears.
- Heading in `ink-content`, body in `ink-muted`.
