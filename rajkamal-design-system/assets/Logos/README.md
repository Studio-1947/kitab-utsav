# Logos

All files are the brand's own SVGs from `kt/assets/logo/`. The ink is `logo-red` (#CB2323) unless noted.

- `rkp-logo.svg`: full logo, round emblem plus Devanagari wordmark, all red. Use on light grounds (`page`, `page-warm`). Desktop and tablet header at the top of the page.
- `rkp-logo-dark.svg`: full logo with a red emblem and white wordmark. Use in dark theme.
- `rkp-logo-text-only.svg`: the logo artwork in red, 827 × 378, for tighter spaces.
- `rkp-logo-text-only-white.svg`: the same in white (#FFFFFF). Use on `footer-bg` and other red or dark grounds.
- `rkp-favicon.svg`: the round emblem alone, red, 64 × 64. The header shrinks to this on scroll. Also the favicon.
- `rkp-favicon-dark.svg`: the emblem for dark grounds.

Never recolour, stretch or redraw the mark. Leave clear space of at least half the emblem's width around it.
